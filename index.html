About Us

VICTIM Skopje is an association founded from the doctoral research of our president, Dr. Dragana Kostevska. It emerged from her deep commitment to understanding and combating the victimization of vulnerable groups, especially the elderly.

Mission: To provide professional support, protection, empowerment and reintegration for identified and potentially vulnerable groups.

History: Established as a result of many years of research by Dr. Dragana Kostevska.
