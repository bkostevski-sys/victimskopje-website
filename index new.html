About Us

VICTIM Skopje is an association founded from the doctoral research of our president, Dr. Dragana Kostevska. It emerged from her deep commitment to understanding and combating the victimization of vulnerable groups, especially the elderly.

Mission: To provide professional support, protection, empowerment and reintegration for identified and potentially vulnerable groups — victims of violence, human trafficking, elderly people, women, children and persons with disabilities.

History: Established as a result of many years of research by Dr. Dragana Kostevska. We have implemented projects such as “Rehabilitation of Female Prisoners” (2021) and “Applied Methods for Prevention of Victimization among Youth” (2022) in Ohrid.
